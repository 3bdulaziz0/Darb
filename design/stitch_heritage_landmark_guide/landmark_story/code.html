District Cathedral

Catedral del Distrito

Built 1872
Gothic Style
Overview
record_voice_over Voice: Layla

The District Cathedral stands as a monumental achievement of late 19th-century gothic revival architecture. Its towering spires were designed not just to reach towards the heavens, but to serve as a navigational beacon for travelers navigating the dense urban expansion of the era.

link Source: Heritage Archive

Constructed entirely from locally quarried limestone, the facade has weathered over a century of city life, acquiring a distinct golden patina that is especially vibrant during the twilight hours. The intricate gargoyles lining the eaves function as part of an elaborate ancient drainage system.

link Official Records

Inside, the nave features some of the largest stained glass rosettes in the region, restored meticulously in 2015. The acoustic properties of the stone interior make it a revered venue for classical choir performances.
